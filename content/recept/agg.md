# Ingredienser
* A sharp object like a thumbtack
* Any number of eggs, preferably ones that aren’t ultra-fresh
* A heavy-bottomed pot
* A pasta fork or slotted spoon
* Water and ice

# Tillagning
1. To start, puncture a hole in the round end of each egg with a thumbtack.
That’s where the air chamber is, and poking a hole in the egg releases pressure inside, so the shell won’t crack.
